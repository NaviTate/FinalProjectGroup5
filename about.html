About Beanfolk

Coffee is more than a drink. It is part of everyday routines, conversations, traditions, and communities around the world.

Beanfolk was created to explore the many ways people experience coffee and to make discovering coffee culture simple, welcoming, and enjoyable.

Our Story

Beanfolk began with a simple idea: coffee culture looks different everywhere, and every place has its own story to tell.

From traditional coffee ceremonies in Ethiopia to espresso bars in Italy and modern specialty cafés in cities around the world, coffee brings people together in countless different ways.

Beanfolk brings these stories together in one place. Our website explores coffee origins, brewing methods, equipment, tips, and coffee culture across different cities and countries.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make coffee knowledge accessible to everyone, whether you are completely new to coffee or already interested in different beans and brewing techniques.
